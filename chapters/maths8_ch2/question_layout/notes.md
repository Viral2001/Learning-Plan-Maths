## Judgement calls

1. This book has no labelled Examples. I counted its in-text "?" prompts, Try This items and blanks that put a task or a "how many" to the reader with a definite answer (even where the book shows the answer right after, as with the stones, the pond and the lock), and did not count exposition: tables, the power line of 4, the halving line, the derivation of 2⁰ and 10⁻³, and "Can we say ...?" lead-ins that the book answers itself. The "?" icon was not treated as decisive in either direction.
2. A block can go to only one question, so where one block holds several printed prompts (C8, C19, C20, C23, C24, C29, C55, C57, C58) they are one question and their ids are folded with `canon`. Ids that belong to the book's statements riding in the same block (neg_base, letters, product_law, power_of_power, same_exp_law, quotient_law, facts, 5900, sci_form, expanded, try_codes, time_table, events_order, why_3p2_3p5) are deliberately not folded, so they stay covered by their teaching concept. Several teaching concepts now carry no ref at all (for example C4, C7, C13, C16, C19, C34, C35, C44, C45), as in Class 8 Ch 6; the gates did not change.
3.
