## Judgement calls

1. All nine Figure it Out items cut from Practice concepts count as `exercise`, including the open ones (the sticks method, the Pacific island "why do you think", the Chinese zong and heng). The kept Questions concepts of this chapter are the same kind (for example "Try making your own number system", "Where in your daily lives ... "), so the layout is consistent. The Math Talk and "?" icons sit beside ordinary questions in this edition, so I judged by substance: a definite answer plus a plan block with the book's own numbers. That is why 1345, the landmark products, the counting board and the Mayan numbers count while the herd questions do not.
2. Naming. No kept concept name carries a page number (the one hit for "page", "A loose page of strange symbols" in the framing topic, is not "page N"), and the structure gate stays at 0. The live Questions concepts carry the Figure it Out number in the form "Theme, Qn", so the new Figure it Out items follow it. Figure it Out sets B (Roman) and H (Mesopotamian) had no existing theme name, so they are "Roman Numerals, Q1" and "Mesopotamian Numerals, Q1". The theme "Place Value" is the live name for the Figure it Out set at the end of 3.4, so Q1 and Q2 take it too.
3. M2.S6.T6.C14 b2 (2367) is not moved: the plan answers MMCCCLXVII, the book prints MMCCCLXII for the same sum (1000 + 1000 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 50 + 10 + 5 + 1 + 1). The plan is arithmetically right and the book numeral is a misprint (LXII is 62); either way the two disagree, so it stays in teaching rather than becoming a question whose answer differs from the printed book.
4. `rename_teach` has one entry: M2.S6.T6.C16 "A second Roman sum: LXXXVII + LXXVIII" is named after the numerals of the question that moved, so it becomes "Regrouping upward through every landmark" (what its teaching block does: five I's make V, two V's an X, five X's an L, two L's a C). No other teaching concept is named after a moved question.
5. The five prompts and the two examples are cut from teaching concepts, so they are named `<Topic short>: <subject>`. Three of those concepts are thin-extra (C16, C35, C45) but still carry objectives and images, so the checker rejects moving them whole; only the worked-example block is cut and each concept stays with its text and teaching blocks.
6. The Practice concepts' live videos (VID1 on the exercise block) solved the Figure it Out item that moved; they now sit on a concept holding only a recap, so they are worth a look at the next video refresh. The 16 new questions have no video yet.
7. Figures (`q_figure`, `s_figure`) are set only where the printed question is a picture the card would otherwise lack: the Egyptian symbol table and numerals (FiOD Q1, Q2), the base-5 Table 2 and numerals (FiOE Q1, FiOF Q2), the Egyptian addition example and FiOF Q1, the counting board, the Mayan symbols and the Chinese rod forms.
