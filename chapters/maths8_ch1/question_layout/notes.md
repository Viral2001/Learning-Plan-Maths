## Judgement calls

1. Where the line is. This edition has no labelled examples and its "?" and Math Talk icons sit beside real questions, so I judged by substance: an in-text item counts when the book poses it to the reader as a question or task (Is ...?, Find ..., Which ..., How many ..., Write ..., Let us check ...), it has a definite answer, and the plan holds a block that uses the book's own numbers. A "Consider N" or "for example, to find" demonstration of a method does not count, even when the book prints the full working (25 and 38, √81, √1936, cubes of fractions). "Is 324 a perfect square?" counts, "Consider a number such as 38" does not.
2. Plan-authored numbers stay. Blocks C2 (locker 12), C6 (side 6 added), C11 (1 + ... + 15), C16 (25 and 36), C20 (area 64) use numbers the book does not print, so they stay in teaching and the printed question goes to Gaps.
3. Instances of a printed general question count when the plan's answer is the book's answer: 36 odd factors (C3) and the 3000² zeros (C9). The 25 and 36 gap count (C16) does not, because the printed question asks for a general rule.
4. Partial cards: C17 (two parts of a ten-row table), C34 (the 3 digit part of "1, 2 and 3 digits", plus the two-zero question) and C37 (4104 only). The card shows what the plan block holds; the missing printed parts are listed in Gaps.
5. Merged blocks and `canon`: only ids that are parts of one moved question are folded: five_nonsquares into ends1, nth_odd into next_square, prime_156 into prime_324, cube_3 and cube_4 into cube_2, cube_00 into cube_count_digits, root_500 into root_3375. Separate printed questions that share a plan block (parity, cube_9, cube_digits, the Math Talk ids, story and statement ids such as taxicab, odd_cubes, diff_squares, root_def, square_def) are left on their teaching concepts; several teaching concepts now hold only text and teaching blocks and a few have no refs left.
6. Tiers: all non-Figure-it-Out questions are `prompt`; no "Example N" name was invented. Names follow the chapter's existing "Topic, Qn" style for Figure it Out items (kept: "Square Numbers, Q8", "Cubic Numbers, Q2") and `<Topic short>: <subject>` for the rest. No name carries "page N", so `rename_teach` is empty (the structure gate passes). The Q numbers in the Figure it Out names are item numbers, not page numbers. Thin teaching concepts named after a moved question (C25 "Test 1156 and 2800 by pairing primes", C42 "Cube roots of 64, 512 and 729", C31 "Akhil's handkerchief") keep their teaching names; the new question names are worded differently, and their objectives (O24, O41, O30) are unchanged.
7. Plan text defect to fix when the images are made: Figure it Out 1.3 option (v) prints "15625 + 512" in the plan; the book prints 15625 + 51². The correct answer (iv) 15625 + 251 is unaffected, so the question was moved, but the question card will show the lost superscript until the plan text is corrected.
8. Figures are set only where the printed question depends on a picture: the triangular-number dots, the difference rows of the cubes, and the kept Q9 tile picture. The cube-building question (C33) was left without one because the printed picture already shows the 2 by 2 by 2 cube, which would give the count away on the question card.
