## How a book question was recognised here

This edition prints a round "?" icon beside every Example and every in-text item put to the reader (checked on the PDF, printed pages 81 to 105), and prints the answers of the in-text items in its solutions section (83, 89, 92, 94 to 97, 99, 101). I used the brief's substance test, not the icon alone: an item counts when the reader is asked to find, write, evaluate, fill or check something with a definite answer and the plan holds a block for it with the book's own numbers. Rhetorical lines answered in the same breath inside a derivation, open tasks and discussion do not count.

Because the Example and its "?" follow-ups are separate printed items, I followed the plan's own split: the block in the teaching concept is the Example; a thin-extra concept whose single block is a "?" follow-up moves whole with its video. A printed question that the plan fragmented (a list of 7 expressions, a card set, a two-row machine figure) is folded into one with `canon`.
