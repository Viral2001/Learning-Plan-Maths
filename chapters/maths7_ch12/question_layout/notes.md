## How a book question was recognised here

Checked on the PDF (pages 67 to 96). In this chapter the "?" icon is printed on nearly every question-like line: labelled Examples, real questions put to the reader, rhetorical lead-ins ("What operation must we use here?", "Can we convert 10/3 ...?") and open investigations. It is not decisive. An item counts when the book labels it (Example, Try This) or puts a task with a definite answer to the reader (compute, complete a table, a yes/no with an example), AND the plan has a worked-example block with the book's own numbers. Left in teaching: rhetorical lead-ins answered in the next line, derivations the book narrates in full with no icon (1325 / 4, four revolutions, the 400th-year count), rule-finding and Math Talk discussion, open investigations. The plan tags every item with an id, so ids say nothing about the book's treatment.

Examples 9 and 11 carry no "?" icon in the printed book but are labelled, so they count.
