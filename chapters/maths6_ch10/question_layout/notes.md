## How a book question was recognised here

The book marks most of its own questions with the round Figure it Out icon (checked on PDF pages 2 to 25), but not all:
several questions that put a blank or a definite question to the reader carry no icon and are answered in the next
line (the Food Court blank, Gurmit, Basant, the Teacher's note question, the lift attendant). I counted an item when the
reader is asked something with one definite answer and the plan holds a block with that question and its answer. The
Math Talk badge (page 11), picture-reading, "why" and "find out" items, and finished equations printed beside a rule
were not counted. Each Questions topic follows the book's order, so some in-text questions sit between Figure it Out
items (Adding Button Presses, Back to the Number Line) or after them (the mine, the token subtraction example).

## Judgement calls

1. Icon-less questions are counted (items 3, 4, 5, 6, 12, 26) because the brief decides by substance and each has a
   definite answer in a plan block. To drop one, delete its entry from the spec; nothing else changes.
2. Tier: unlabelled narrated questions (Gurmit, Basant, the attendant) are `prompt`. `example` is used for the book's
   printed "Example", "More examples" and "Examples" and for the three posed demonstrations (items 16, 21, 22). The
   plan's own ids (ex1 for Gurmit) were not used for the tier.
3. Names: the book's examples are unnumbered, so new names use `<Topic short>: <subject>` rather than "Example n".
4. Item 29 folds the yes/no follow-up (10.2.q2, thin-extra C61) into the example it follows (canon `10.2.q2` to `10.2.ex3`),
   as one printed question. The alternative is two questions; C61 has its own live video, which is folded away.
5. Renames. The image titles are built from the concept name and the structure gate refuses "page N", so the 30 existing
   Figure it Out concepts named "10.x, page N, Figure it Out ..." would have added 60 structure failures. They are given
   page-free names in the Class 8 style ("Adding Button Presses, Q1", "Token Subtraction, Q3", "Temperature, Q2")
   through `rename_teach`, which the engine applies to any concept id. The 19 kept names that carry only "p263" style page marks
   ("Figure it Out (box 1) Q1, p263", "Figure it Out Q1, p268") pass the gate and were left. C19 "Who is on the lowest floor?" is renamed "Lowest floor from floor numbers"
   because its question moved out under that name.
6. Figures: `q_figure` and `s_figure` are set where the printed question depends on a drawing: the Building of Fun (the
   names top to bottom from page 3, no numbers), the vertical floor line (A to H), number lines, unmarked number lines,
   token rows, the cross-section graph, the Leh table, the border grids, the circle-strike grids and the string of
   tokens. The cross-section heights in `s_figure` are the plan's approximate answers. Text-only questions have none.
7. Several moved blocks carry the book's narrated working inside their `question` text (C34 b2, C62 b2, C63 b2 and, to a
   lesser extent, C59 b2), so the question card would show the method. The engine moves them as written; trim the
   question text when the solutions are authored.
8. After the cuts, 23 teaching concepts keep their text, teaching plan and own video but lose their worked example.
   Teaching concepts whose example is not a book question (C2, C3, C5, C33, C39, C78, C88, C96, C120) are untouched.
