## Which items to flip
Candidates: items of `out_<CHUNK>.json` with `decision` `none` or `optional` whose `reason`, `reason_codes` or notes say the figure is already a teaching image: `REUSES_CONCEPT_IMAGE`, or words such as "teaching image", "concept image", "already shows", "already its concept's image", "same-concept image", "kept image". Also read each `notes_<CHUNK>.md` (the Borderline and decision sections) and the `figure_inventory` entries mapped to `concept_image:<media_id>` whose figure is also what an item of that concept would need.

For each candidate decide: **if no teaching image existed, would v3 make this item `required`?** Apply Steps B, B2, C and E of `HERE/spec/diagram_identification_prompt_v3.md`: the learner needs the figure to ATTEMPT the question (the question gives a figure); the solution adds something visual or its printed or LP answer IS a figure; a worked example's steps rely on the figure; a lettered or referred figure.
- YES -> flip to `required` (below).
- NO (all data is in the text, a plain table, a text-only answer, the figure would only repeat the question diagram, and so on) -> leave it exactly as it is. The teaching image was then not the reason.
- Keep the one v3 exception: the 'refer picture' question side (the book's answer only points at a picture that is a concept image of an EARLIER topic) stays `optional` with its `text_alternative`. Do not flip those.
Do not flip items that are `none` for other reasons (LaTeX table, arithmetic, OPEN_ENDED, DECORATIVE_ONLY, NOT_RENDERABLE). Never touch a teaching image or any block link of it.
