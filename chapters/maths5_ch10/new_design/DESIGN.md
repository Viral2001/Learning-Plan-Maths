## 1. Hard rules (gates enforce most; fix content, never a gate)

Characters and settings are the book's: Prem, Manu, Lali; letters, digits, the windmill firki, designs of squares, triangles and circles, block prints, vegetable blocks. The plan's own WE use the same kind of things. Do not invent facts or datasets.
- **No em dash and no en dash** in any string or comment (the book's dashes become commas, colons or "to"). Minus is U+2212 `−`. Use `×` and `÷`. Hyphen only inside words ("three-quarter turn", "2-, 3- and 4-digit" are fine).
- **Fractions are written `a/b` with a plain slash and no spaces** (`1/4`, `3/4`). Never the characters ½ ¼ ¾. Never glue a letter to a fraction. A turn is written "1/4 turn", "1/2 turn", "3/4 turn" or "full turn" (the book's words, with the fraction first). "a quarter turn", "a half turn" and "a three-quarter turn" are also fine in prose. ## 4. Verified answers (computed with Python; use exactly these)
**Opener letters (plain sans-serif).** Can be cut by drawing half or a quarter: E (1/2, horizontal fold), X (1/4), T (1/2), K (1/2, horizontal fold), V (1/2), O (1/4); N cannot (no line of symmetry). Horizontal line: E, X, K, O. Vertical line: X, T, V, O. Both: X, O. N looks the same after a 1/2 turn (link to the turning table). Diya and boat: each has a vertical line of symmetry; fold in half, draw half, cut, open (sample).
**Firki.** The firki has 4 equal blades; it looks the same after 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and a full turn; the dot moves but the firki looks the same.
**Turn table.** H (printed): 1/4 no, 1/2 yes, 3/4 no, full yes; rotational symmetry yes, at 1/2 turn. I: same pattern, yes at 1/2 turn. X: yes after every turn, rotational symmetry yes, at 1/4 turn. Y: only after a full turn: rotational symmetry no.
**Digits (1 drawn as a bar; 3 and 8 read with equal halves; measured on the printed digits: 0 99% on both lines, 8 97% on the vertical and 87% on the horizontal, 3 67% on the horizontal).** Reflection: 0, 1, 3, 8. Rotational: 0, 1, 8. Both: 0, 1, 8. 6 and 9 turn into each other, neither looks the same by itself. 2, 4, 5, 7 have neither. 11 and 1001: both symmetries (vertical line between the middle, a 1/2 turn keeps them). Examples: rotation only 69, 619, 6969; reflection only 38, 383, 3883 (horizontal line); both 88, 808, 8118.
**Making Designs.** (a) No (the circle at the right tip has no partner on the left). (b) add a circle at the left tip: same after a 1/2 turn. ## 6. Misconception ids (defined in content.py; attach by id)
M_ANY_LINE (any line through the middle of a shape is a line of symmetry, even when the halves do not match), M_FULL_TURN (every shape has rotational symmetry because it looks the same after a full turn), M_DOT_MOVES (if the dot moves after a turn, the shape looks different), M_BOTH_ALWAYS (a shape that has one kind of symmetry also has the other), M_ONE_LINE (a symmetrical shape has just one line of symmetry), M_SIX_NINE (6 and 9 have rotational symmetry because a half turn turns one into the other), M_HALF_IMPLIES_QUARTER (a design that looks the same after a 1/2 turn also looks the same after a 1/4 turn), M_COLOUR_IGNORED (colours do not matter when checking symmetry), M_FLIP_IS_TURN (turning a shape by a 1/2 turn is the same as folding it over a line).
