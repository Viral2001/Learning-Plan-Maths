## Judgement calls

1. The Math Talk and "?" icons sit beside both real questions and pure discussion here (Q5, Q6, Puneeth, the coffee table, Activity 2). I did not treat an icon as decisive: I moved an item when the reader is asked something with a definite answer and the plan has a block with that question, its steps and its answer. Two inline questions that the book answers in its own running text (image D against A, p160; the simplest forms and the factor from A to D, p161) are counted as prompts because they are put straight to the reader with a definite answer and the plan holds the question, steps and answer; the "why" discussions beside them are not.
2. C3 b2 and C8 b2 leave their teaching concepts with text and teaching blocks only, like several Ch 6 concepts. Their videos stay on the teaching concept; the new questions have no video yet.
3. Activity 3 is counted although it is a hands-on activity: its questions have definite numeric answers and the plan block holds them, and the live plan already gives Activity 1 a question concept. Activity 2 is not counted, because the plan block is not the printed question (call 1 above is the test).
4. Names follow this chapter's existing style: "Example: <subject>" with no book number (as the existing Example concepts for Ex 4 and Ex 5), "Activity: <subject>", "<section>, Qn" for Figure it Out items (sections: Ratios and Proportions, Rule of Three, Ratio Sharing, Unit Conversions) and "<Topic short>: <subject>" for in-text prompts. No name carries a page number or "Fig."; the existing kept names carry only the "Qn" of their Figure it Out set, as in Ch 6, and the structure gate shows 0 problems before and after, so no rename was needed for that reason.
