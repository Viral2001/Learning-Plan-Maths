## Judgement calls

1. Doubled Figure it Out content. In this plan every Practice concept holds all the Figure it Out items of its section as `exercise` blocks (with the question, steps and answer), while the Questions topic already has a video-only concept for the same item (placeholder block, empty question, an objective). I kept those concepts exactly as instructed and cut out only the items that have no Questions concept: Q1 of each section, plus FiO2 Q2, FiO5 Q3 and the Real Life Q1. The duplicate blocks (for example C6 b2 to b6, C36 b2 to b9) stay in the Practice concepts as in the live plan. Folding them into the kept concepts is not possible with the engine today: `whole` is refused for a concept that carries an objective. So the kept question concepts have no question text of their own, and their `q_figure` / `s_figure` (written from the book page and the plan's solutions in the Practice blocks) are all the image step gets. A later step could attach each Practice block to its kept concept and drop it from the Practice concept.
2. Plan-authored numbers stay. Blocks C4, C5, C13, C14, C20, C21, C22, C27, C28, C34, C35, C45, C46, C51, C52 and C61 use numbers the book does not print (the book prints figures without numbers there, or asks the reader to find their own example), so they stay in teaching and the printed question goes to Borderline or Gaps. C2, C3, C12, C19, C49, C50, C59 and C60 use the book's own numbers and moved.
3. Tier and names. No labelled examples in this edition, so in-text questions are `prompt` and named `<Topic>: <subject>`; Figure it Out items are `exercise` in the kept style "Topic, Qn". "Areas in Real Life, Q1" is a prompt named in the kept Q2 to Q5 style.
4. The trapezium grid question merges two blocks from two concepts because they are two figures of one printed question; the numbers for both are counted off the grid (the plan wrote them into its blocks). trap.wxyz is folded into trap.grid; trap.formula stays on C50. Alternative: leave C50 b2 in teaching as the plan's own example; I did not, because its numbers are the printed grid trapezium's.
5. A4 and conversions are partial cards. The A4 block pairs the printed 21 by 29.7 with the plan's own 3 by 4 table (the book's tabletop is a personal estimate); the conversion block lacks 7.4 in and 5.08 cm. Each shows what the plan block holds.
6. FiO2 Q1 and Q2, and FiO5 Q1 and Q3, land in different Questions topics although the book prints each set together, because the plan keeps them in different Practice concepts (rule: the topic the block sits in). Book order is kept inside each topic.
