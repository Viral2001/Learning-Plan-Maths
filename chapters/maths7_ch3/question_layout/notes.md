## How a book question was recognised here

This edition prints the answers of its in-text questions in a solutions section (checked on the PDF, pages 36 to 55), and puts a round "?" icon (sometimes Math Talk or Try This) beside the questions it puts to the reader. A plan block was moved when all three hold: the book marks the item with the icon or prints its answer, the item has a definite answer, and the block carries the book's own numbers and a correct answer (I checked each moved answer against the printed solutions and figures). Lead-in questions the book answers in the very next line ("How many cm is 5 mm?"), discussion, and statements the plan turned into a task stay in teaching.
