## How a book question was recognised here

The round icon of this edition is printed on narration as well as on questions (checked on PDF pages 75 to 105: it sits on "Naresh and Navya decided to go to each student...", on the Example, on "Answer the following questions", and on the Math Talk question), so it decides nothing. The test used: the reader is asked something with a definite answer, the plan holds a block with that question, its steps and its answer, and the book either prints an answer for it (its Example solutions on p80, its solutions section for p83, p86 and p93) or the answer is read straight off a printed figure or table. The book's solutions section confirms the in-text items on p83, p86 and p93; the others were judged on substance.

The plan tags every in-text question as a worked example (4.1.q1 to 4.5.q5), so its ids say nothing about whether the book treats an item as a question. The Questions topics here are the Class 6-7 shape: enrichment concepts "Figure it Out p.N Qn" with no objective, kept as they are. The "Practice:" concepts hold only a recap block, so nothing is cut from them.
