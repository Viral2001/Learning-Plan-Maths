## How a book question was recognised here

The chapter prints no labelled "Example". Its worked material is a set of tasks posed in the running text ("Construct a triangle ... ", "Does a triangle exist with ... ?", "Find the third angle ... ") that the book then works out in full, plus the Construct box and the Figure it Out sets. The "?" icon in this edition sits next to real tasks and next to plain discussion lines alike (checked on the PDF, pages 146 to 172), so it was not used as the test. A plan block was cut out when (a) the book puts that specific problem to the reader with the same numbers or figure, (b) it has a definite answer, and (c) the block holds the question, its steps and that answer. A plan example with its own numbers stays in teaching. Because no item carries an Example label, every new question is tier `prompt`.
