## How a book question was recognised here

In this edition the round "?" icon sits beside every question put to the reader: each Example, each Figure it Out
header, the in-text follow-ups ("If the total number of friends goes up to 7 ...", "If another friend, Sangmu, joins ...",
"Use this method to find ...") and also open or explanatory items ("Take some more expressions and check", "Can you
explain why ... using the Token Model", "Which one should she wear first?"). So the icon is not decisive. An item counts
when it has a definite answer and the plan holds a block with the book's own numbers. A plan worked example that
retells such an item (names changed, numbers the same) is cut out. Items with no single answer, narrated derivations
that the book answers in the next line, and the plan's own examples stay in teaching.

Placement: examples and in-text prompts go first in the Questions topic of the concept topic they sit in, then the
existing Figure it Out concepts (the 2.1 Figure it Out is printed before Examples 2 and 3 in the book, but the plan keeps
them in different concept topics, so Example 1 comes before it and Examples 2 and 3 open the next Questions topic).
