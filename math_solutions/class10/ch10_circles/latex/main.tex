\subsection*{Properties and formulas used in this chapter}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(P\arabic*),leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Tangent--radius property.} The tangent to a circle at a point is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.
\item \textbf{Equal-tangents property.} The lengths of the two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.
\item \textbf{Centre on the angle bisector.} If two tangents are drawn from an external point, the line joining that point to the centre bisects the angle between the tangents. (Proof: the two right triangles formed with the centre are congruent by RHS.)
\item \textbf{Perpendicular from the centre to a chord} bisects the chord.
\item \textbf{Pythagoras theorem.} In a right triangle, $(\text{hypotenuse})^2=(\text{base})^2+(\text{perpendicular})^2$.
\item \textbf{Angle-sum property.} The angles of a triangle add up to $180\deg$; the angles of a quadrilateral add up to $360\deg$.
\item \textbf{Heron's formula} (used in Ex.\ 10.2 Q12). For sides $a,b,c$ and $s=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2}$, area $=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$.
\item \textbf{Area of a triangle with inradius $r$.} Area $=\dfrac12\times r\times(\text{perimeter})$.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Layout of a proof.} Every proof is written as Given, To prove, Construction (if needed), then a Step / Statement / Reason table. The reason column names the property used in words.
